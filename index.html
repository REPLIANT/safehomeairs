SERVICES

Inspection, testing, and consulting tailored to your property

We support residential and commercial clients with services focused on identifying problems, improving clarity, and helping you move forward with confidence.

Mold Inspections

Detailed inspections designed to identify hidden moisture issues, locate potential mold growth, and document the conditions affecting your property.

Air Quality Testing

Indoor air quality testing for homes and businesses concerned about airborne mold spores, humidity, odors, or ongoing respiratory discomfort.

ERMI Testing

Advanced environmental testing options for clients who want deeper data about indoor contamination concerns.

Leak Detection

Moisture and leak detection services to help uncover the water sources that may be contributing to mold growth behind walls, ceilings, or flooring.

Mycotoxin Testing Support

Guidance for clients looking for next steps when mold exposure may be affecting health, including support around mycotoxin testing resources.

HVAC Inspection

HVAC evaluations to determine whether the system may be spreading contamination or contributing to poor indoor air quality.

Environmental Consulting

Professional environmental consulting focused on identifying contamination sources, reviewing indoor conditions, and guiding the next steps toward a healthier indoor space.

Mitigation Remediation Consultation

Property-specific recommendations and support so you can move forward with the right remediation strategy instead of guesswork.

WARNING SIGNS

Signs your indoor environment may need attention

You notice symptoms improve when you are away from the home or building.
There is staining, spotting, or unusual discoloration on walls, ceilings, or furnishings.
A musty or earthy smell appears after rain, humidity, or air conditioner use.
Indoor humidity levels stay elevated for long periods.
You see dark buildup around vents, registers, or HVAC components.
Allergy-like or respiratory symptoms seem worse indoors.
